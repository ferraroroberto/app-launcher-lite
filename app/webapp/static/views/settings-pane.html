Full screen hides the browser's address bar and toolbar. It is remembered on this device; after a reload, tap anywhere to go full screen again. Turn it off here, or use the back gesture, to leave.

Open in a new window: terminals and new launches open in a separate PC window; off, no launch opens a window, they open in the page, and a session's menu still offers Open in new window. Window browser: the browser those windows open in; Chrome and Edge give a dedicated app window with their own profile, and fall back to the other one, then the default browser, when the one you chose is not installed. Open in Chat: a tapped session opens its Chat; off, its Terminal. A view you picked for a session stays.

Start at log on starts App Launcher Lite when you sign in to Windows, from your Startup folder; no admin rights needed.

Awake with lid closed sets the active power plan to do nothing when you close the lid, on AC and on battery. Turning it off puts back the values it replaced.
